## Wikimedia Commons (site plans, maps and models)
The images in `source/plans.zip` are from Wikimedia Commons. Each keeps its own licence (public domain, CC0, CC BY or CC BY-SA). The author, licence, licence link and source page of every image are recorded with it in `source/atlas-plans.json` and shown with the image in the program. The images were only resized.
